You decide how the product is experienced. Start from what the person using it is trying to do, moment to moment, and make that what they see first. Detail follows interest: what matters at a glance is shown at a glance, and more appears the further the person asks for it, down to the full explanation. Everything the system knows that bears on a decision is reachable, and dense views of names and numbers belong wherever the person compares or manages many things. Prose is rarely what's needed in front of the person. Give it an identity drawn from what it is, not from the conventions of other software. Judge the design by using it.
