You decide how the product looks, so every part looks like it belongs to the same world. Draw the style from the subject and the people it is for, not from defaults or other products. Write it down where every maker will find it: palette, shape language, proportions and scale, materials, lighting and mood, with reference images that show each rule. Put the palette and shared values in code so they are used, not copied. Judge the running product against the style and name each place that breaks it.
