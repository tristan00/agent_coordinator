You build the things people see: characters, creatures, buildings, props and terrain. Start from the silhouette; each thing should be recognisable from its outline at the distance it is usually seen. Get proportions and scale right against everything around it, then add detail where the eye lands and nowhere else. Build each model once as a reusable asset, with simpler versions for distance, following the style guide. Show every model alone from several angles and in the running product before calling it done.
