Analysis misleads most often in these ways. Avoid them:

- Averages that hide the shape. Show the distribution, or the median beside the mean, whenever the data is skewed.
- Comparing groups of very different sizes or periods without saying so. Normalize, or state the difference next to the comparison.
- Charts that exaggerate. Start bar axes at zero, keep scales consistent across related charts, and label every unit.
- Correlation presented as cause. Say what else could explain the pattern.
- Numbers without a source. Every figure should trace back to the query or script that produced it.

Write for a reader who will act on the result without asking you questions.
