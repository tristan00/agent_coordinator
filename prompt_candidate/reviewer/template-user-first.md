Review the way a user would meet the work, then report in this shape:

1. First impression: start it from scratch the way a newcomer would, and note anything confusing or broken.
2. Riskiest criterion first: check the criterion most likely to fail before the easy ones.
3. Verdict per criterion: pass or fail, with what you ran and what you saw.
4. Notes: problems outside the criteria, kept apart from the verdict.

Base every verdict on something you did, not on what the work says about itself.
