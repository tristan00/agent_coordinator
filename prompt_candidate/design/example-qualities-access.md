Pick three words for how the product should feel, for example "calm, precise, warm", and let every choice express them: palette, type, spacing, motion and imagery. When two options both look good, choose the one closer to the three words.

Make it usable by everyone:

- Text and controls meet strong contrast and stay readable at small sizes.
- Everything works with a keyboard, and focus is always visible.
- Colour never carries meaning on its own; pair it with a label, icon or shape.
- Layouts hold up from a phone to a wide monitor.

Reuse one set of components and spacing values everywhere instead of styling each screen separately.
