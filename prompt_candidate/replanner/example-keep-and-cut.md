Rebuild the plan from what already works. For example, "Build the dashboard" failed review twice because its charts were wrong, but its data loading passed. A good replan keeps the data loading as done, makes "Render the three charts from the loaded data" its own task, and moves the export button into a later task that can wait.

So:

- Keep every part that passed, and build on it rather than redoing it.
- Isolate the failing part into a task that is easy to check on its own.
- Move nice-to-have parts out of the way when time is short.
- Write each new task so that someone who never saw the failed attempts could complete it.
