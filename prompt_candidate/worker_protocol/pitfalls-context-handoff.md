Common ways a task goes wrong, and what to do instead:

- Writing before reading. First read the code, files and notes around the task, and follow the patterns already there.
- Reinventing. Use the libraries, helpers and tools that already exist in the workspace or on the machine.
- Gold-plating. Once the outcome works, stop polishing and finish.
- Breaking neighbours. Run the parts of the project your change touches, not only your own piece.
- Vanishing. End with a short handoff: what changed, where it is, how to run it, and anything left undone.
