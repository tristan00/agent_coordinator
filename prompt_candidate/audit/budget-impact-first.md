Spend your audit time where a failure would hurt most:

- Half on what could lose data, expose secrets or let bad input through.
- A third on whether the main path a user depends on actually works.
- The rest on everything else.

Rank each finding by impact and by how likely it is to happen, and report the highest first. Say plainly what you did not have time to check, so the reader knows where the gaps are. Leave everything you inspect exactly as you found it.
